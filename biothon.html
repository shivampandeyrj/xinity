Biothon 2026

STATUS: COMPLETED TEAMS: 390 PARTICIPANTS: 1200+

Biothon 2026 is an interdisciplinary innovation sprint and hackathon hosted by Marwadi University (MU) in Rajkot, Gujarat. The event bridges the gap between computational systems, biomedical engineering, and life sciences research to develop real-world solutions for challenges in healthcare, agriculture, and environmental biodiversity.

Event Highlights

  • Total prize pool of up to ₹1.8 lakhs
  • Core domains: IT/Computer Science, Biomedical Engineering, and Life Sciences
  • Multi-stage competition culminating in an offline Grand Finale on July 22, 2026

The Xinity Impact

Xinity provided the end-to-end infrastructure for Biothon 2026. From managing the initial project presentation (PPT) submissions in Round 1 to handling 5-7 minute video walkthrough uploads in Round 2, the platform kept everything organized for the judges leading up to the Grand Finale.
