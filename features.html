The Best Way to Evaluate Skills

Xinity provides the precise tools you need to host competitions, track participants, and score projects fairly. We've built the complete loop for judging any skill-based talent, from coding to financial analysis.

For Corporates

Host internal hackathons, case studies, or external skill challenges. From registrations to the final pitch, Xinity keeps all your event data in one place so you can focus on evaluating ideas.

For Colleges & Universities

Built to handle mass events across any domain. Easily manage hundreds of student registrations, organize teams, and assign judges seamlessly without relying on manual spreadsheets.

For Job Hiring

Resumes only tell half the story. Hosting a technical, financial, or specialized challenge is the best way to judge a candidate's real problem-solving ability and performance under pressure.

Custom Judging Criteria

Every event is different. You can define exact judging criteria (e.g., Innovation, Financial Viability, UI/UX) and securely assign specific judges to specific rounds within the Admin portal.

Live Rankboard

Keep the excitement high. As soon as a judge submits a score from their portal, the public Rankboard updates instantly, ensuring everyone knows exactly where they stand without any manual refreshing.

Participant Portal

Participants get a clean interface to form teams, track their progress, and upload their presentation files directly, avoiding last-minute email attachments.
